About

I'm Tomás — pronounced 'too-mah-sh'. Design Director with fifteen years across brand identity, digital and product. Most of my work has been in sport. I move between brand and product more than most. That's not accidental — the best digital work I've done has always been rooted in a strong brand foundation.

I direct and I make. I have a team and I rely on them, but I like staying close to the work. I think you give better direction when you still know how to do the thing yourself.

Currently leading the digital team at TwelfthMan, a sport-focused design agency in London. Also Creative Director of Contrast — a clothing brand from Lisbon I co-founded and rebranded last year. New identity, new website, socials and three physical stores. I once failed the entry test to become a football manager. Became a designer instead — probably the right call.

Currently Design Director, TwelfthMan — London
Creative Director, Contrast — Lisbon
Elsewhere Lifelong Benfica fan. Two European finals with my dad. I ride a red bike called Eusébio and photograph random lost chairs when I need a break from screens.
